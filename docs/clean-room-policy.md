# Clean-Room Policy

Inforsight must be safe to publish and independently defensible.

## Allowed inputs

- Fictional data and procedures created for this project.
- Public standards, public documentation, and properly cited public actuarial or engineering references.
- Original source code and tests written for this repository.
- Open-source dependencies used in accordance with their licenses.

## Prohibited inputs

- Confidential, proprietary, or customer data from an insurer or employer.
- Internal source code, schemas, rules, prompts, procedures, screenshots, logs, documents, or terminology.
- Real names, policy numbers, addresses, contact details, payment information, or credentials.
- Material copied from non-public systems, even if identifiers are removed.

## Review rule

If the provenance of an input is unclear, do not add it. Open a decision issue, describe the uncertainty without including the material, and choose a fictional or public alternative.

## Synthetic-data labeling

Generated datasets must include a prominent synthetic-data notice, generator version, seed, creation time, and assumptions reference. Sample data should remain small enough for manual inspection.
