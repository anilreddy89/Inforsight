# Inforsight realism boundary

This document defines what the current reference implementation models, what it
simplifies, and what it intentionally excludes. RH-12 uses this boundary when
describing corrected evidence.

## Modeled

- Fictional Generation v6 policy-event histories with dual-time, point-in-time
  observations.
- A bounded 90-day combined lapse-or-surrender risk target.
- A frozen release model bundle and calibrated risk scores.
- Synthetic potential outcomes for the canonical conservation actions.
- RH-02 eligibility results under the sealed fictional cohort's explicit
  clear-state safety facts.
- RH-04 signed combined-termination effects, exact USD micros, personnel
  seconds, and the one-year annual-premium value basis.
- RH-05 portfolio allocation with deterministic tie-breaking, abstention, and
  fixed total budget/personnel capacities.
- Policy-cluster resampling under the two estimands recorded in the
  [RH-12 evidence manifest](experiments/phase-rh-12-evidence-reconciliation-1.0.0.json).

## Simplified

- Action effects are generated by the fictional counterfactual simulator; they
  are not observed treatment responses.
- The value basis treats one avoided combined termination as one year of the
  source annual premium. It is not profit, margin, cash value, or realized
  revenue.
- The corrected procedure uses frozen scores, effects, and economics during
  resampling. It does not refit or recalibrate the predictive model.
- The current synthetic counterfactual contract has a 90-day horizon but no
  intervention timestamp or event-time attribution, so lead-time metrics are
  not supported.
- The bounded allocator is validated exactly only over its declared small
  fixture domain; the large-cohort procedure is reported as a bounded local
  reference behavior, not a global optimality result.

## Excluded

- Real carrier records, customer data, credentials, or proprietary procedures.
- Causal treatment-effect identification or randomized intervention evidence.
- Realized customer outcomes, profit, premium revenue, or customers saved.
- External validity, actuarial credibility, regulatory approval, or production
  readiness.
- Demographic fairness, disparate-impact, or equality-of-odds conclusions; the
  synthetic corpus intentionally contains no demographic attributes.
- Macroeconomic shocks, product features outside the fictional term/whole-life
  surface, and governed production servicing evidence.
- Network, sustained-load, distributed durability, KMS authenticity, and
  enterprise cloud performance.
- Final-holdout access. RH-12 records `not_accessed` for the final holdout.

## Required claim language

| Topic | Allowed description | Prohibited shorthand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk | Synthetic 90-day predictive risk ordering/calibration | Real-world accuracy or actuarial validity |
| Effect | Signed modeled combined-termination effect | Causal uplift or lapses prevented |
| Value | Modeled expected annual premium preserved under RH-04 | Profit, revenue, realized value |
| Allocation | Conditional procedure behavior under RH-05 capacities | Globally optimal or production-ready allocation |
| Queue | Modeled expected-effect recall at capacity | Observed intervention recall |
| Fairness | Fairness assessment not supported | Fair or unbiased deployment |

Historical Phase 3.08 artifacts retain their original bytes and labels. The
versioned RH-12 artifacts add corrected context; they do not erase the earlier
result.
