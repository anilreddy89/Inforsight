## RH-05 portfolio allocation

- Activates portfolio-allocation contract `1.0.0` in the bounded local runtime.
- Applies one deterministic, feasibility-preserving allocation before dashboard recommendations, briefs, and workflow cases are emitted.
- Uses exact integer USD micros and personnel seconds, stable occurrence/action tie-breaking, and explicit abstention.
- Matches an independent exact reference with zero additive gap across the declared generated small-instance sweep; no global-optimality claim is made outside that domain.
- Rebinds dashboard allocations to cutoff, portfolio membership, model/economics identity, capacity version, and reservation version. Refreshes create new allocation identities.
- Atomically releases and replaces local reservations under an expected capacity version. Stale versions and insufficient capacity fail without partial resource changes.
- Reports non-intervention, operational rules-only, risk-ranked, and allocation-engine strategies against one frozen comparison context. Results are synthetic conditional modeled values, not causal, realized, or production-performance estimates.
